About the technology

Vitali's training engine analyzes recorded clinical sessions end-to-end: it transcribes the team's walkie-talkie audio, aligns it with the timestamped actions and process metrics captured in each Circle, reconstructs what happened, and compares the run against best-practice benchmarks built from thousands of similar cases. From that comparison it generates the timestamped coaching you see here — and when a session is exemplary, it extracts the transferable lesson and distributes it to every nurse who handles similar cases. The pipeline is designed to scale to large volumes of real-world workflow data across hospital systems.
